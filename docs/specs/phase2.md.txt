Read AGENTS.md, docs/data_contracts.md, docs/phase_handoff.md,

Continue the project. Do not rebuild Phase 1.

GOAL

Turn the U.S. grid into a geographic feature dataset.

This phase belongs to the geography component.
Do not rank locations or invent sustainability scores.

INPUT

data/processed/us_grid.parquet
Existing schemas and configurations.

CORE SOURCES

Implement adapters for:

- USGS Annual NLCD: land-cover composition.
- EPA eGRID: historical grid-emissions intensity.
- WRI Aqueduct 4.0: baseline water-risk indicators.
- NOAA/NCEI climate data: historical cooling-related conditions.
- FEMA flood data: flood-hazard screening.
- Wildfire Risk to Communities: wildfire screening.
- EIA/U.S. Energy Atlas: energy-infrastructure proximity.
- An authoritative protected-area dataset: ecological screening.

Locate official documentation and download endpoints.
Verify fields, units, geography, coverage, and access requirements.

DATA ACCESS

Use cached downloads and batch geospatial processing.

Do not issue an unnecessary separate network request for every cell.
Do not download unlimited nationwide high-resolution data without
estimating volume and supporting bounded processing.

Support local-file inputs when a source requires credentials,
manual access, or a large external download.

Never bypass access controls or fabricate unavailable data.
Mark adapters and data coverage as READY, PARTIAL, or BLOCKED.

FEATURE ENGINEERING

For each cell derive, where supported:

land-cover fractions
potentially suitable land fraction
protected-area overlap
grid-emissions intensity
eGRID subregion association
baseline water stress
historical temperature summaries
flood-hazard overlap
wildfire indicators
distance to mapped energy infrastructure

Preserve source values before normalization.

Use spatial intersections or zonal statistics when appropriate.
Do not assign all heterogeneous land and hazard information from
the cell centroid alone.

When a cell intersects multiple source regions, record that fact.
Store documented summaries and source-region shares where useful.
Do not imply a blended screening value is a confirmed site value.

Keep source no-data masks distinct from genuine zero values.
Unmapped flood coverage is UNKNOWN, not zero flood risk.

Aqueduct ratios, scores, categories, and missing-value codes must
remain distinguishable. Do not assume every field is a 0–1 index.

NO DECISION LOGIC

Land-cover composition is a geographic feature.
Whether it satisfies a project's requirements belongs to the model.

Do not exclude an entire cell merely because a small portion
overlaps water, wetlands, or protected land.

Record overlap and coverage so the model can evaluate usable areas.

PROVENANCE

For every metric preserve:

grid_id
metric_name
value and units
source and source URL
source field
data year or period
retrieval date
native spatial resolution
aggregation method
coverage fraction
status
missing-data reason

A long-form provenance table may accompany the wide feature table.

LIMITATIONS

- Infrastructure proximity is not confirmed power capacity.
- Aqueduct is not a water-supply commitment.
- Climate normals are not an hourly operating-year simulation.
- eGRID historical averages are not automatically future or
  marginal emissions.
- Source resolution must remain visible after joining to the grid.

- Every Phase 1 cell is retained or accounted for explicitly.

- Unit conversions and spatial joins have tests.
- Missing information remains missing.
