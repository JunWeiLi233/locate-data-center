PROJECT GOAL

Search the contiguous United States geographically to identify
potential regions for sustainable data-center construction.

The system must have exactly two main components:

1. geography:
   U.S. grid generation, data ingestion, and geographic features.

2. model:
   Screening, physical calculations, decision analysis,
   clustering, and validation.

Do not build a frontend, chatbot, autonomous research-agent system,
or machine-learning location predictor.
