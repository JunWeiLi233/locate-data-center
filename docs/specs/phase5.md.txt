INTERPRETATION REQUIREMENTS

Solar/wind resource is not contracted electricity.

Queued generation is not guaranteed future supply.
Do not convert queue MW directly into future emissions intensity.

Preserve EIA-861 reporting definitions and comparability.
Do not mix interruption metrics with incompatible reporting bases.

FCC availability is not proof of diverse data-center fiber routes.

CWNS proximity is not a reclaimed-water supply agreement.

Material plant technology/proximity is not a product-specific EPD.

FAF regional flows are not an exact delivery route.

Historical cyclone exposure is not a future landfall probability.

Future climate grids are not parcel-scale engineering assessments.

periods, not an accidentally inclusive 26-year sum.

report the gap or an explicitly labeled extension assumption.

CONSTRUCTION AND LIFECYCLE MODEL

Where evidence supports it, calculate:

material emissions =
quantity × compatible product emissions factor

transport emissions =
mass × distance × compatible mode emissions factor

Track material units and accounting boundaries.
Avoid counting transport twice when an EPD already includes it.

Report construction, equipment, operations, replacements, and
end-of-life separately.

Missing lifecycle components are UNKNOWN, not zero.
Label partial totals accordingly.
