These are configurable examples, not industry requirements.

SCREENING

Each requirement returns PASS, FAIL, or UNKNOWN, with evidence
and a reason.

Support:

STRICT:
An unknown critical requirement prevents acceptance.

EXPLORATORY:
Unknown requirements retain a conditional candidate, visibly flagged.

Never turn an unknown requirement into a pass.

Evaluate explicit requirements for land, ecological exclusions,
hazards, water strategy, and infrastructure.

Thresholds must come from configuration with a stated rationale.
Do not invent scientific or regulatory limits.

Separate:

potentially suitable total area
largest contiguous suitable area
confirmed developable parcel area

If contiguous land cannot be established from available data,
leave that requirement UNKNOWN.

Do not add scattered suitable pixels and call them a buildable parcel.

POWER AND CONNECTIVITY

Keep confirmed utility capacity and data-center-grade fiber
verification separate from geographic proximity proxies.

Power-capacity checks require peak facility demand.
Do not use annual-average IT demand as the required connection size.

If peak facility demand or utility availability is unverified,
record that limitation.

PHYSICAL CALCULATIONS

Use these explicit units:

E_IT_MWh =
peak_it_power_mw × average_it_load_factor × hours_in_modeled_year

E_facility_MWh =
E_IT_MWh × PUE

C_electricity_tonnes =
E_facility_MWh × grid_carbon_kg_per_MWh / 1000

W_site_liters =
E_IT_MWh × 1000 × WUE_liters_per_IT_kWh

If supported:

W_electricity_liters =
E_facility_MWh × 1000 × grid_water_liters_per_kWh

Keep water consumption distinct from withdrawal.

Do not apply local water stress to all electricity-related water
unless its generation-water geography supports that treatment.

COOLING CONFIGURATIONS

Represent a complete cooling design, including heat transport
and heat rejection, rather than treating "liquid" as a complete
water-consumption specification.

Allow documented engineering functions or explicit PUE/WUE
scenario assumptions.

If climate-dependent relationships are unavailable, use a clearly
labeled constant-assumption scenario. Do not claim that it models
geographic cooling differences.

Never generate engineering coefficients from an LLM guess.

Produce one performance record per:

grid_id + design_id + scenario_id

Do not mix the best electricity result from one design with the
best water result from another.

OUTPUTS

screening_results.parquet

updated methodology and handoff
